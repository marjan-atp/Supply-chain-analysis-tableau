📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

An interactive Power BI Sales Analysis Dashboard built to analyze sales performance, profitability, regional performance, customer contribution, sub-category performance, and shipping distribution.

🖼️ Dashboard Preview

[Sales Analysis Dashboard](Dashboard.png)



🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform sales data into an interactive business dashboard that helps users quickly understand:

Overall sales and profit performance

Profit margin and year-over-year growth

Top-performing customers

Regional sales contribution

Profit trends over time

Sales and profit by sub-category

State-level profit performance

Quantity distribution by shipping mode

📌 Key KPIs

The dashboard highlights the following business metrics:

KPI

Description

Total Sales

Overall sales generated

Profit Margin

Profitability relative to sales

Total Profit

Overall profit generated

Sales YoY Growth %

Year-over-year sales growth

Sales Last Year

Previous-year sales for comparison

📈 Dashboard Visuals

1. Top 3 Customers by Sales

Identifies the customers contributing the highest sales value.

2. Regional Sales

Shows the contribution of different regions to overall sales.

3. Profit Trend

Tracks how total profit changes across years.

4. Sales & Profit by Sub-Category

Compares sales and profit performance across product sub-categories.

5. Top 10 States by Profit

Highlights the states generating the highest profit.

6. Quantity Distribution

Shows the distribution of product quantity across different shipping modes.

🎛️ Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically filter the dashboard using:

City

Category

State

The visuals update automatically based on the selected filters.

🗂️ Dataset

The project includes supply_chain_20k.csv, containing 20,000 records and 24 columns related to products, sales/revenue, inventory, suppliers, manufacturing, shipping, transportation, and costs.
