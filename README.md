📊 Supply Chain Dashboard – Tableau

An interactive Supply Chain Dashboard built in Tableau to analyze revenue, profitability, product sales, inventory turnover, defects, supplier performance, demand, and inspection results.

🖼️ Dashboard Preview

![supply chain Dashboard](dashboard.png)

🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform supply chain data into an interactive Tableau dashboard that provides a clear view of operational and financial performance.

The dashboard helps analyze:

Total revenue and profit margin

Products sold

Defect rate

Inventory turnover

Stock vs. demand over lead time

Revenue by product type

Products sold by location

Supplier defects

Inspection results

📈 Dashboard Visualizations

1. Stock vs Demand Over Time

Compares stock/order quantities across different lead times to understand how supply changes with demand and lead-time patterns.

2. Revenue by Product Type

Compares total revenue generated by:

Cosmetics

Haircare

Skincare

3. Products Sold by Location

Compares total products sold across:

Bangalore

Chennai

Delhi

Kolkata

Mumbai

4. Inspection Results

Shows the distribution of inspection outcomes across:

Pass

Fail

Pending

5. Supplier Defects

A heatmap comparing supplier defect levels across different product types.

6. Interactive Filters

The dashboard allows users to filter the analysis by:

Location

Supplier Name

💡 Business Insights

The dashboard can help businesses identify:

Which product types generate the highest revenue

Locations with higher product sales

Supplier and product-type combinations associated with defects

The proportion of products passing, failing, or awaiting inspection

Relationships between lead time, stock, and demand

Overall profitability and inventory performance

🚀 How to Use

Clone or download this repository.

Open Supply_Chain_Dashboard.twbx using Tableau Desktop.

If required, reconnect the workbook to supply_chain_20k.csv.

Open the dashboard.

Use the Location and Supplier Name filters to explore the data.
